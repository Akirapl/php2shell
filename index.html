CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619: critical RCE flaws in the PHP core

Independent, sourced live record of PHP2Shell — CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, two critical (CVSS 9.2) remote code execution flaws in the PHP core. Patch status: released 21 Oct 2026, 13:34 UTC — fixed in PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35. Every fact links to its source.

As of 23 Oct, 18:20 UTC (20:20 CEST): PHP2Shell patches are out (8.5.12 / 8.4.27 / 8.3.36 / 8.2.35). PHP warns of a new, unpatched flaw CVE-2026-115560 in a rare configuration — mitigation: session.upload_progress.enabled=0. Attack attempts (including Mirai botnets) continue; no confirmed compromises.Attempts: AWS (MadPot) saw the first on 17 Oct, during the embargo, more from 21 Oct 02:00 UTC; Cloudflare: thousands before the announcement, “millions now”. Outcomes: no confirmed compromises (Recorded Future, heise 22 Oct); watchTowr and Defused say circulating exploits mostly do not work. Not in CISA KEV (checked 22 Oct, 10:27 UTC).
